Posterior Analytics

Aristotle on demonstration, scientific knowledge, and first principles, in two books.

This edition presents the original Greek (Ross (OCT, 1964)) side by side with English translations (G. R. G. Mure, E. S. Bouchier, O. F. Owen) — with click-to-parse morphology, dictionary entries, and exact Bekker citation. 2 books, 53 chapters.
