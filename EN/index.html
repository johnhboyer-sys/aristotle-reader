Nicomachean Ethics

Ἠθικὰ Νικομάχεια

Aristotle’s central work of moral philosophy, in ten books.

This edition presents the original Greek (Bywater (OCT, 1894)) side by side with English translations (H. Rackham, W. D. Ross, Martin Ostwald, F. H. Peters) — with click-to-parse morphology, dictionary entries, and exact Bekker citation. 10 books, 116 chapters.
