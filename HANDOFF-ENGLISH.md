# HANDOFF: English text corrections

Written 2026-11-03. Branch `claude/english-corrections`, pushed; the pull request
holds commits `0e01179`, `d7cf97d`, `76f071f` and the one adding this file.
Not deployed. Tools and the checkers' notes are in `docs/english-sweep/`.

## 1. Goal

Find and fix every OCR and copying error in the site's public English
translations, each checked against a scan of the printed book. John's rulings:
no translation is removed from the site; every error is fixed; Ostwald waits.

## 3. Decisions and why

- **Fix the sources, not the parser.** The repo's rule is that misprints are
  fixed in the HTML and listed in `english-corrections.json`; furniture went
  the same way, so `stage1_ross` is unchanged and every other text parses as
  before.
- **Restore the archive's own text where it exists** (Cael, Mete) rather than
  the checkers' transcription, so spelling and conventions match the page.
- **Remap rather than regenerate** hand-kept anchor files: they may hold John's
  hand edits. Only files whose generator hash was intact were regenerated, and
  on main the tool reproduced all 19 of those byte for byte first.
- **Keep each map's existing length drift.** Most gloss maps are one character
  short of today's parse (the parser's trailing newline came later);
  `gloss_map_to_anchors.py` tolerates 4, so `ross_len` moves by each chapter's
  change, not to its new length.
- **Socrates stays out of Pol-public's proper names** until a stage 2 run
  shows the name check passes now that 1316a/1316b have English.

## 4. Checks that matter (and their controls)

- `apply/verify.py`: applying every record as plain find-and-replace to the old
  parse gives exactly the new parse, in all 163 books. A one-word change is
  caught.
- `apply/anchors_check.py`: every anchor that resolved before resolves at the
  mapped place now. A moved anchor is caught.
- Paragraph files: the build's own `_load_paragraphs` accepts the remapped
  files; main's files fail against the corrected text (79, 57 and 45 entries).
- Rulings: `align.rulings.locate` finds each at its mapped offset.

## 5. What did not work

- Re-downloading the cut pages: the archive itself is cut (see above).
- A character-level diff for remapping hung on 30,000-character chapters;
  `apply/wordmap.py` diffs word tokens instead (4 seconds for all maps).
- `apply_real.py` places a fix by visible text; ten whose find spans a
  paragraph or chapter break needed `handfix.py`, and On Winds 973b's dots
  landed after a `</p>` (`vent_fix.py`).
- One record (Mirab 83, a stray full stop at a chapter start) needed the end of
  the previous chapter in its find: chapters join with a space, so the bare
  `. In Crete` matched across the join.
