Metaphysics

Aristotle’s inquiry into being, substance, and the unmoved mover, in fourteen books.

This edition presents the original Greek (Ross (OCT, 1953)) side by side with an English translation (W. D. Ross) — with click-to-parse morphology, dictionary entries, and exact Bekker citation. 14 books, 142 chapters.
