But when the dough is taken out and kneaded again, the smoothest part of the flour and the stickiest part of the moisture being left mingle more with one another, owing to the fact that they have become smoother and stickier, and owing to the effect 25of the heat; for their mixing resembles the process of dyeing, so that the dough, when subsequently kneaded, is like boiled flour.
