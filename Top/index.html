Topics

Aristotle’s manual of dialectical argument and the topoi, in eight books.

This edition presents the original Greek (Ross (OCT, 1958)) side by side with English translations (W. A. Pickard-Cambridge, O. F. Owen) — with click-to-parse morphology, dictionary entries, and exact Bekker citation. 8 books, 83 chapters.
