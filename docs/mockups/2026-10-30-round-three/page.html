The Aristotle Reader: round three

Three directions now. The diagrams are gone. B takes the style you liked: one early photograph with the title set over it in large red capitals that let the photograph show through. A and D are as before, with your notes applied. Every page is drawn at desktop and phone width side by side. Keys: 1 to 3 pick a direction, the arrows step through the pages, t switches to dark, p switches between both widths, desktop and phone.

What changed after your notes

  • “No diagrams. Too gimmicky.” C is dropped. The division of the works might return later as a side page, as you suggested, but not as the home page.
  • “Too many photos on homepage.” B’s home page has one photograph, not forty-two. The site would show a different one at each visit; here “Next photograph” steps through them. The works below it are a plain list.
  • “No need for photos on every page.” Photographs appear on the home page and on work pages only. Search and the lexicon have none.
  • “No pictures on reader pages.” None in any direction. D’s Aldine page is gone from the reader too.
  • “These books need to be collapsible.” On every work page the books are closed by default, one line each. A book opens to its chapters, and “Open all books” opens them together.

Round two stays where it was, with your notes on it: round two ↗. Round one: round one ↗.

The same in all three

  • Chapters lead: the work pages list every chapter, the reader opens each chapter with its number and title, and its running head names the chapter you are in. Bekker numbers stay in the margins.
  • Click a Greek word and its parse opens in a sidebar on the right; on a phone it comes up from the bottom.
  • The LSJ entry stays Grammata’s; here a stand-in, since an artifact cannot load the module.
  • Greek and English at one size, in one ink. Each keeps its own Bekker numbers.
  • Every work is on the home page, and every work has its own page.
  • Search, the lexicon, compare, Greek / Both / English, footnotes, text settings, Bekker jumps, light and dark.

What the mockup leaves out

  • Only Book I of the Ethics opens in the reader. Every other work’s page opens from the home pages.
  • Search shows one query, ἀρετή in any form; the lexicon shows ἀρετή.
  • Phrases, Support, Texts & Licences and the other readers are placeholders. Print cannot run inside an artifact, so its button does nothing here.
  • Interface copy and image captions are drafts.
