# Round seven: what John said about round six

Round six showed four new directions: E Spectrum (a colour per work, no
pictures), F Objects (museum objects in colour on a dark wall), G Type (type
alone; first shown with a giant glossed Greek sentence opening each page) and
H Plates (one old print per work, in one blue ink beside the title).

His notes, in his words:

- On G: "G's home, work, and the big quote on reader page are out."
- "H is in running but we might need to pick different plates."
- "E is too basic. Not a bad idea, but too bland with just colors."
- "F doesn't work. The photos end up not working because they don't match
  palate."
- "Either B or D or H…but maybe not plates that are so renaissance? Idk."
- "Or we just clone Plato's current look with the opening text and a
  different palate…"

B is round five's Poster direction (red titles over black-and-white
photographs); D is round four's Aldine direction (each work beside its first
printing, Venice 1495, EB Garamond). Round seven puts B, D, H and a clone of
the Plato Reader's design side by side.

His standing rules (from every round) are in README.md under "What every
direction must do"; read BRIEF.md for the full history.
