## Concrete defects

Count: six defects, affecting rows 1, 2, 9, 27, 39 and 49. No Aristotle chapter-containment defect found.

1. **D1 — Row 1, reason: the argument’s direction needs correction.** PDF 24 (printed 7) says VI 3 and XI 8 “both take it as obvious that not every thing happens of necessity”. Sorabji then argues that some causal chains must stop before the present or past: “should not stretch back as far as the present or past”. PDF 25 (printed 8) states the resulting denial that causal ancestry always reaches the past. The row describes an outright argument for indeterminism by tracing chains to the present. In Sorabji’s reading, that tracing gives the deterministic consequence to avoid; the accepted indeterminism supports the break in the chain.

2. **D2 — Row 2, range: the enclosing span excludes a parenthetical citation.** PDF 23 (printed 6) matches `Metaph. VI 2, 1026b22-4`. The row starts its enclosing range at **1026b27**, five lines later. The repo gives VI 2 as **1026a33–1027a28**, so the cited b22–24 belongs to the correct chapter, but falls outside the row’s enclosing range. This is an internal range defect, not a wrong-chapter defect.

3. **D3 — Row 9, reason: “the only necessity in nature” is too broad.** PDF 162 (printed 145) rejects unqualified formulations of that claim and distinguishes being from coming to be. PDF 163 (printed 146) cites `Phys. n9`, `200a1; a16; a19`, expressly “about coming to be”, then interprets a13 and a30–32 as hypothetical necessity. The same page allows other necessities, including everlasting natural kinds and recurring changes; PDF 164–167 adds causal necessities. The reason should restrict the claim to the passage’s treatment of natural coming to be and acknowledge Sorabji’s qualification.

4. **D4 — Row 27, first Sorabji chapter: not found for the supplied lines.** Searched ch. 2, PDF 43–61, for Physics II 7 and `198b5–9`, including shortened/noisy forms. PDF 59 cites `Phys. 11 7, 198a19`, which lies outside the row’s span. The supplied lines occur at PDF 68 (printed 51), `Phys.n7, 198b7-8`, in **ch. 3**, and PDF 165 (printed 148), `n 7, 198b5-6`, in **ch. 9**. Ch. 2 treats the same Aristotle chapter, but the row’s particular citation is not found there.

5. **D5 — Row 39, first Sorabji chapter: not found for either supplied span.** Searched ch. 2, PDF 43–61, for DA II 4, `415a14–22` and `416a9–18`, including shortened/noisy forms. Its DA II 4 citations instead read `415b2 and 20` at PDF 57 and `415b21-3` at PDF 59. The supplied lines occur at PDF 183 (printed 166), `n 4, 415a14-22`, in **ch. 10**, and PDF 168 (printed 151), `DA n4, 416a9-18`, in **ch. 9**; PDF 185 also cites `416a9-19`.
