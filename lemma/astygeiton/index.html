Dictionary (LSJ)

ἀστυγείτων, ον, gen. ονος, A. near or bordering on a city, σκοπαί A. Ag. 309; πόλιες Hdt. 6.99, cf. 9.122, E. Hipp. 1161, Plu. Rom. 23; πόλεμοι Arist. Pol. 1330a18. 2. as Subst., neighbour to the city, borderer, Hdt. 2.104, 5.66, Th. 1.15, X. HG 1.3.2., SIG 633.10 (Milet., ii B.C.), etc.
