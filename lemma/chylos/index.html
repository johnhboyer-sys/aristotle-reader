Dictionary (LSJ)

χυλός, ὁ, (χέω) juice in general, opp. ὕδωρ, Ocell. 2.3, cf. Iamb. in Nic. p.81 P.; in various uses: I. juice of plants, χυλῶν στακτῶν εἴτε ἀνθῶν ἢ καρπῶν Pl. Criti. 115a, cf. Arist. HA 596b15, Col. 796a23, Aud. 802a15, Thphr. HP 6.4.6, LXX 4 Ma. 6.25. b. decoction, Dsc. Eup. 1.55; but distd. fr. ἀφέψημα, Id. 1.105. 2. of animal juices, Hp. Alim. 11, 14, Arist. Col. 794a21. 3. juice produced by the digestion of food, chyle, Gal. UP 4.3. 4. barley-water, gruel, having the barley or groats strained off, whereas πτισάνη was taken unstrained, πτισάνης χ. Hp. Acut. 6, cf. Cratin. 297 (lyr.), Ephipp. 13.6 (anap.): pl., Anaxipp. 1.46. II. = χυμός II, flavour, taste, Gorg. ap. S.E. M. 7.85, v.l. in Arist. EN 1118a28; αἱ διὰ χυλῶν ἡδοναί Epicur. Fr. 67, cf. Ep. 3. p.63U., Diocl. Fr. 112, Phld. Mus. p.103K.; κατʼ ὀσμὴν καὶ χρόαν καὶ χυλόν Id. Sign. 27: metaph., χ. στωμυλμάτων, φιλίας, Ar. Ra. 943, Pax 997 (anap.). (Gal. 11.450 distinguishes χυλός juice fr. χυμός flavour, attributing this usage to Aristotle and later writers, whereas earlier authors used χυμός in both senses: the Mss. vary.)
