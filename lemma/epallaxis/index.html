Dictionary (LSJ)

Metaph. 1011a33, Insomn. 460b20, Pr. 958b14; linking together, Id. Mete. 387a12. 2. overlapping of species, Id. GA 732b15; confusion of different things, Str. 12.8.2. b. alternation, Pl. Sph. 240c. 3. change, θέσεως Hierocl. in CA 1p.419M.; διαιτημάτων Gal. 6.59 (pl.); varieties of abnormal constitutions, ib. 385 (pl.).
