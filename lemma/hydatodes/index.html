Dictionary (LSJ)

ὑδατώδης, ες,
watery, οὖρον Hp. Prog. 12, cf. Epid. 1:4:10 [1.26.ιʹ], Sor. 1.59, al.; opp. αἱματώδης, Arist. HA 586a29; [ἄνεμος] ὑ. Id. Mete. 364b21; [νέφος] -έστερον ib. 377b6; of signs of the Zodiac, Vett.Val. 6.4; ὑ. κρύσταλλος, of melting ice, wet, sloppy, Th. 3.23; of taste, watery, insipid, Thphr. HP 4.10.3.
II. full of water, φύλλα Id. CP 2.19.2; σφαιρίον Id. HP 3.7.5.
2. dropsical, Hp. Epid. 6.7.4.
