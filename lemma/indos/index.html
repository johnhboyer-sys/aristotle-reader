Dictionary (LSJ)

Ἰνδός, ή, όν, Indian, Hdt. 3.38, al., cf. A. Supp. 284; esp. of the drivers of elephants, Phylarch. 36 J., Plb. 1.40.15, al.
2. Ἰνδός, ὁ, the river Indus, Hdt. 4.44, etc.
3. name of a fallacy, Plu. Sanit. 2.133b.
II. as Adj., = Ἰνδικός, Indian, AP 9.544.1 (Addaeus).
2. Ἰνδή, ἡ, (sc. ἔμπλαστρος) name of a plaster, Orib. Fr. 88.
