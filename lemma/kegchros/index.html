Dictionary (LSJ)

κέγχρος, ὁ (also ἡ, Arist. Ph. 250a20, Dieuch. ap. Orib. 4.7.15, Glauc. ap.POxy. 1802.42, Dsc. 2.97, Gal. 6.791, Jul. Or. 3.112a, Iamb. VP 24.106), A. millet, Panicum miliaceum, usu. in pl., Hes. Sc. 398, Hdt. 4.17, Hp. Acut. 21, X. An. 1.2.22, etc.: sg., Hecat. 154 J., Hdt. 1.193, Thphr. HP 1.11.2, al., OGI 55.15 (Telmessus, iii B.C.); of a single grain, Hdt. 3.100, Plot. 6.3.11, prob. in Sapph. Supp. 1.13:—also κέρχνος, Anaxandr. 41.27, Gal. 18(1).574; cf. κέρχνωμα, κέρχνη. II. anything in small grains: 1. spawn of fish, Hdt. 2.93. 2. small beads, Ath. 12.525e. 3. speck, sty in the eye, Adam. 1.11, al. III. = κεγχρίας II (q.v.), Dsc. Ther. 15. IV. small kind of diamond, Plin. HN 37.57.
