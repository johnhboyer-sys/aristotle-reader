Mem. 3.3.14; κ. εἰς τὴν Αἴγυπτον venture thither, Pherecr. 11. b. abs., make a venture, take a risk, Hdt. 3.69, Ar. Eq. 1204; to be in dire peril, Th. 3.28, 6.33, etc.; to be in danger, Arist. EN 1124b8, etc.; of a sick person, Hp. Aph.
