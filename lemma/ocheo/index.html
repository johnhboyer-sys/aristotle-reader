Lex. 2: Aeol. pres. part. ὀχήμενος Lyr.Adesp. 51: in Att. Prose, used only in pres. and impf.: Hom. never uses the augm.: [the first syll. is made long in Pi. O. 2.67, Euph. 9.13, Lyc. 64,1049, where it is written ὀκχέω (Pi. and Euph.) or ὀγχέω (Lyc.), cf. ὄχος 1.1, ὄφις sub fin.]:—Frequentat. of ἔχω, as φορέω of φέρω (ἔχειν τε καὶ ὀχεῖν Pl. Ra. 23; of a general, let the men ride, X. Eq.Mag. 4.1.
