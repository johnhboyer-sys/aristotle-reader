Dictionary (LSJ)

ὀψοφάγος, ὁ, A. one who eats delicacies, such as fish and other dainties, epicure, gourmet, Ar. Pax 810, Cephisod. 9, Antiph. 190.5, Eub. 88, Arist. EN 1118a32; ὀ. εἶ καὶ κνισολοιχός Sophil. 7, cf. X. Mem. 3.14.2 sq., Timae. 71: epith. of a fish, Opp. H. 1.141: irreg. Att. Sup. ὀψοφαγίστατος X. Mem. 3.13.4, Poll. 6.37.
