Dictionary (LSJ)

φυσητήρ, ῆρος, ὁ, instrument for blowing, blowpipe or tube, φ. ὀστέϊνοι Hdt. 4.2, cf. Opp. H. 4.463. 2. bellows, LXX Jb. 32.19, Poll. 10.147, Gal. 2.717. 3. blow-hole or spiracle of whales, etc., Arist. HA 566b3; the funnel through which the cuttle-fish squirts its ink, ib. 541b17. II. one who blows a pipe or bellows, Dsc. 5.75 (v.l.), Suid. s.v. ἐξέλιπε. 2. a kind of whale, perh. Biscay whale, Str. 3.2.7.
