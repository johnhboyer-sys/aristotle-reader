Dictionary (LSJ)

σωλήν, ῆνος, ὁ (also σωλῆνος, ὁ, Anan. Oxy. 1087.57),
channel, gutter, Archil. 5, Aen.Tact. 18.6, PPetr. 2p.119 (iii B.C.), Ph. Bel. 91.26, 28.
2. pipe, Hdt. 3.60, Hero Spir. 1.1, al., Arr. Epict. 4.11.9; κεραμεοῖ σ. Plu. Cupid. 2.526b; σκύτινος Str. 16.2.13; μολίβδινος Gp. 10.18.6; ventilation-pipe, ib. 2.27.2; ἀργυροῦς καὶ χρυσοῦς (for unguents) Plu. Galb. 19 (pl.).
3. cylindrical box for keeping a broken limb straight, Hp. Off. 14, Fract. 16, 22.
4. grooved tile, IG 42(1).109 iv 116, 117 (Epid., iv B.C.), 11(2).203 B 97 (Delos, iii B.C.), Hsch., etc.
5. a shell-fish, perh. the razor-fish, Epich. 42, Sophr. 24, Philyll. 13, Arist. HA 528a18, 548a5, al., Gal. 6.734.
6. membrum virile, Hsch.
7. the cavity of the spine, Poll. 2.180.
8. grooved rails in which wheels run, Hero Aut. 2.2; σωλῆνες καταπαλτῶν, σκορπίων σωλῆνες, IG 2(2).1628.512,515, 1629.986,990.
9. vulgar name for lienteria, Steph. in Gal. 1.314 D.
