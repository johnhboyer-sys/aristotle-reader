Dictionary (LSJ)

συμβιβάζω, Causal of συμβαίνω,
bring together: Pass., to be put together, to be knit together, framed, ἔκ τινος Ep.Eph. 4.16, Ep.Col. 2.19.
2. metaph., bring to terms, reconcile, Hdt. 1.74; σ. τινά τινι reconcile one to another, Th. 2.29; σ. [τινὰς] εἰς τὸ μέσον, as mediator, Pl. Prt. 337e: abs., bring about an agreement, IG 1(2).57.24.
II. put together, compare, examine, τὰ λεγόμενα Pl. Hp.Mi. 369d; [τὰς μεταφοράς] Phld. Rh. 1.174 S.; σ. περί τινων ὃ ἕκαστον εἴη Pl. R. 504a (though Timaeus here expld. it intr. agree).
III. elicit a logical consequence, infer (cf. συμβαίνω III.3 b), Arist. Top. 155a25, SE 181a22; ἔκ τινων Id. Top. 161b37; σ. ὅτι . . ib. 154a36; σ. ὡς . . Id. Rh.Al. 1426a37, 1441a6; σ. πότερον . . Id. Top. 158b27; πῶς . . Phld. Rh. 1.172 S. (Pass.); ὅτι . . Act.Ap. 9.22: c. acc. et inf., Ocell. 3.3:—Pass., συμβιβασθέντος when the conclusion has been drawn, v.l. in Arist. SE 179a30.
2. teach, instruct, συμβιβάσω ὑμᾶς ἃ ποιήσετε LXX Ex. 4.15; συμβιβάσεις τοὺς υἱούς σου ib. De. 4.9, cf. Is. 40.14, 1 Ep.Cor. 2.16.
