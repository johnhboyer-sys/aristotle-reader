NA 2.42. 3. τελεστικόν, τό, payment for admission to a priesthood, OGI 90.16 (Rosetta, ii B.C.). 4. τελεστικά, τά, name of a ceremony, dub. l. in Jahresh. 26 Beibl. 17 (Ephesus).
