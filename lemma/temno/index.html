P. 3.68 and v.l. in O. 13.57, cf. τάμνω ib. 12.6, B. 5.17, 16.4, but is the only Att. pres., Th. 3.26, IG 1(2).76.56, etc. (v. also τμήγω): Iterat. τέμνεσκον A.R. 1.1215, Q.S. 6.217: fut. τεμῶ E.
