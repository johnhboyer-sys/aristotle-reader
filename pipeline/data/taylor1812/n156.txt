CHAPTER I.
IN the first place, it is necessary to determine what noun and verb are;
and in the next place, what negation, affirmation, enunciation, and a
sentence are. Those things, therefore, which are in the voice, are sym-
bols of the passions of the soul; and those things which are written are
symbols of the passions in the voice. And as there are not the same let-
ters among all men, so neither are there the same voices, or articulate
sounds. The passions of the soul, however, of which these are prima-
rily the signs, are the same among all men; and the things of which
these are the similitudes are also the same. Concerning these, therefore,
we have spoken in the treatise On the Soul; for these belong to another
